> [!NOTE]
> 
> Anything within `${` and `}` is a placeholder, and anything within `?{` and `}` is optional.

## Actions

#### Syntax
```
${action name with "ing"s and values} does this:
    ${runnable code}
```

#### Example

```
Kicking a ball does this:
    Say "I am kicking a ball".
```

```
Kicking a [the thing you are kicking] does this:
    Say "I am kicking a " and the thing you are kicking.
```

```
Kicking a [the thing you are kicking] does this:

```
